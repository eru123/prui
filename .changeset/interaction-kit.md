Interaction kit: 14 micro-interaction components, adapted from interior.dev's MIT patterns in prui idiom (tokens + CSS transitions, no Motion dependency).

New components: `CopyButton`, `HoldToConfirm` (+`useLongPress` hook), `IconMorph`, `OTPInput`, `PasswordStrength` (+`scorePassword`), `FloatingLabel`, `ExpandingSearch`, `SegmentedControl`, `Slider` with magnetic `detents` (prui's first slider), `WizardSteps`, `ContextMenu`, `Lightbox`, press-feedback kit (`Ripple`/`PressDepth`/`LikeBurst`), and `SkeletonSwap` (anti-flash loading swap).

Polish: `Tooltip` group timing (tooltips opened soon after another skip the delay), `Avatar` gains a `presence` dot (online/busy/away/offline).
