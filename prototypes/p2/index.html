i.

Drawn from practice

Not a template bought from a marketplace. Every component earned its place in HRLabs, jianpms, icanhelp, or t4xlabs first.

ii.

Versioned, not forked

One package, semantic versions. An improvement lands everywhere with a bump, and drift stops being a maintenance tax.

iii.

Quiet by design

The system stays out of the way. Tokens carry identity; components carry structure; neither shouts.
